## If these are reused for the Play listing

Google asks for phone screenshots between 320 px and 3840 px on a side, with
the longest side no more than twice the shortest, PNG or JPEG. Capture from a
phone (or an emulator at a phone resolution) rather than scaling the desktop
window — a desktop aspect ratio will be rejected. At least two are required;
the store shows up to eight.

Nothing here should contain a real person's name, a real e-mail, or anything
identifying: these files are public.
