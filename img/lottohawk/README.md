## Screenshots

Captured from the real Android release build, holding **made-up tickets**
entered for the screenshots and checked against real published drawings
(Powerball 21 October 2026, Mega Millions 20 October 2026). They are the ones
on the app's Google Play listing. Android's navigation bar is cropped off and
the sides padded by 11 px so the long side is at most twice the short one,
which Google Play requires.
