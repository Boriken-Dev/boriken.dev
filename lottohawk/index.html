LottoHawk

Log your lottery ticket once. Get told if it wins.

Español · Boriken Dev

What it does

Add a ticket once, by typing its numbers or scanning it, and LottoHawk checks it against the published results on its own, after every drawing it plays. If it wins, you get a notification. If it does not, it moves quietly to "not a winner", and you never have to look again.

  • The prize for every winning line, including add-ons such as Power Play, Megaplier, Wild Ball and FIREBALL
  • Where to claim a prize in the state you bought the ticket in, in the lottery's own words, with its claim centres
  • How many days are left to claim, before a winning ticket expires
  • Tickets with several lines, and tickets that play several drawings

Games

Powerball, Mega Millions, Lotto America and Millionaire for Life, and the draw games of the US states, the District of Columbia, Puerto Rico and the US Virgin Islands: Pick 3, Pick 4, Cash 5, Take 5, Pega 3 and many more. Scratch-off tickets are not supported: only the lottery can check those.

Private

No account with us, no ads and no tracking. Your tickets, numbers and photos stay on your phone; LottoHawk downloads published winning numbers and sends your tickets nowhere unless you ask a lottery to check one. The privacy policy says exactly what the app keeps and where it connects.

LottoHawk is independent: it is not affiliated with any lottery, and it sells no tickets and takes no bets. What it shows is unofficial; always confirm a win with the lottery or a retailer before you discard a ticket. For adults 18 and over.
