Your own lineup, from any live stream, watched like television.

What it does

LineupMux takes whatever live streams you have and makes stations of them: an antenna or cable tuner on your network, IPTV playlists, HLS and multicast streams, internet radio, a camera at your front door. Each station gets its number, name and logo; you mix them from every source into lineups of your own, and browse them with a programme guide, with the remote, like television.

The app carries its own server, so it works by itself on the TV, with no computer to set up. If you already run a LineupMux server at home, it connects to that, or to several at once.

Any kind of stream

  • Network tuners, including HDHomeRun and SAT>IP
  • IPTV playlists (M3U) and HLS streams, and the public iptv-org channel index
  • Multicast and UDP streams, and cameras that stream over HTTP
  • Internet radio and audio-only streams, and the Radio Browser directory
  • Programme guides from your sources, or from any XMLTV guide

A stream your TV cannot play as it is gets converted with the device's own hardware codecs, and only then.

On Android TV

Your lineups can be inputs of the television's own channel list and rows on its home screen. Link Remote pairs a phone with a short code, to drive the television and set it up from the couch.

Private

No account with us, no ads and no tracking. Your servers, sign-ins and settings stay on the device. The privacy policy says exactly what the app keeps and where it connects.

LineupMux comes with no channels or content of its own: you need your own tuner, playlist or other source, and the right to watch what it carries. Protected (DRM) streams are not supported.
