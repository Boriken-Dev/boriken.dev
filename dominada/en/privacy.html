Dominada does not collect, transmit or sell personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics and no advertising.

What is stored, and where

Your games, the players you add, the teams you name, your rule profiles and your preferences are stored only on your device, in the application's own data area. They are not sent to any server: the application has no server.

Player names are whatever you type. Nicknames are fine; the app does not care. A player's optional link is a label you type so a shared game can recognise the same person — it is not an account, it is not verified, and it is not checked with anyone.

When you share a game

Sharing a game is yours to do and happens only when you ask. When you do, the app hands it to whichever app you choose — messaging, email, a file — and from there that app's terms apply, not ours.

What travels in that copy is the game: the rule profile, the hands recorded and the names of who played, plus a player's link if you set one. Nothing else.

Permissions

Dominada keeps the screen awake while a game is open, so it does not sleep in the middle of a hand.

If counting pips with the camera is added later, the camera will be used only when you ask, the photo will be processed on your own device, and it will not be stored or sent anywhere. This page will be updated before that reaches the application.

Children

The application asks nobody for personal information, children included.

Deleting your data

Deleting a game in the app deletes it from your device. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Since nothing is ever sent anywhere, there is nothing else to ask us to delete.
